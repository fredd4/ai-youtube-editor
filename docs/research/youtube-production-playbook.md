# YouTube Production Playbook — Research Report (Sept 2026)

Research target: a Polish-language travel-vlog channel, 16:9 long-form (10–20 min), phone footage, solo creator, aiming for high retention and viral potential.

**Sourcing note:** the MrBeast "How to Succeed in MrBeast Production" document is a leaked, unverified internal memo (Aug–Sep 2024). Treat its rules as strong heuristics. Several 2026 "best practice" sources are marketing blogs; soft numbers are flagged.

---

## 1. MrBeast's production philosophy — encodable rules

Sources: leaked PDF (mirror: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623bf84e83241ec49b548e4/66edaa19db6e9359bb92931f_How-To-Succeed-At-MrBeast-Production%20(2).pdf), https://www.danielscrivner.com/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production-summary/, https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/09/17/mrbeast-internal-production-guide-leaked-key-points/

**Three metrics govern everything:** CTR, AVD (average view duration), AVP (average view percentage).

**Master arc:** "Hook people at the start, transition them to an amazing story they are invested in, have no dull moments, then a satisfying payoff with an abrupt ending."

| Window | Job | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | Hook + validate the thumbnail/title promise | Most important minute. ~35% of viewers drop here even on a great video. |
| 1:00–3:00 | "Crazy progression" | Don't narrate linearly; compress time aggressively to build investment. |
| ~3:00 | Re-engagement #1 | Spectacle beat placed where attention naturally dips. |
| 3:00–6:00 | Peak window | Most exciting, simplest content, rapid scene changes. Viewers who reach the half will mostly finish. |
| ~6:00 | Re-engagement #2 | Second spectacle, pushes into back half. |
| 6:00–end | Back-half lull | Longer explanations, less-critical content live here. |
| End | Payoff + abrupt cut | Never telegraph the ending. |

Other hard rules:
- Avg video length ~13:37. Shorter for emotional/real-time content.
- **Thumbnail–content congruence is literal** (colours, objects must match; mismatch causes measurable dropout).
- **Lighting quality in the first minute correlates with lower early dropout.**
- Title extremity beats tone accuracy ("I Survived…" > "I Spent…").
- "No dull moments" is absolute. Never lose the "wow".
- Stop-down CTAs/sponsor reads crater retention; narratively integrated ones barely dent it.
- Authenticity: "We don't fake things."
- Intro re-scripted until the premise lands in <5 s with zero preamble; expect ~30% loss in first 30 s regardless.

**Travel-vlog translation:** replace money-spectacle with place-spectacle and risk/consequence. Plan the 3:00 and 6:00 beats at shoot time (drone reveal, police stop, price reveal, unidentifiable food, sleeper-bus disaster).

## 2. Retention & structure for travel vlogs

**Cold open:** drop into the most dramatic/curious moment first, then rewind. Highlights montage: tight, under 10 s. No branded intros.

**Recommended skeleton (16:9, 10–20 min, Polish narration):**
- 0:00–0:07 cold open: best single shot + one line stating the premise. No logo, no "cześć, witajcie".
- 0:07–0:25 promise + stakes; show the thumbnail moment (or close cousin).
- 0:25–0:35 first deliberate pattern interrupt (angle, push-in, text card, sfx).
- 0:35–3:00 crazy progression: compress arrival/logistics ruthlessly unless something goes wrong.
- 3:00 re-engagement #1. 3:00–6:00 peak window. 6:00 re-engagement #2.
- 6:00–end slower reflective sections, cost breakdowns, conversations.
- Last 20 s: payoff then abrupt cut; final 5–20 s reserved for end screen.

**Pacing:**
- Modern average shot length ~2.5 s. Change something visual every 10–15 s minimum.
- Travel vlog target: 2–4 s avg shot length in 0:00–6:00, 4–7 s after. Shorts-style 0.5–1 s cutting is wrong for long-form.
- B-roll ratio ~60/40 B-roll to A-roll; no piece-to-camera longer than 8–10 s without a cutaway.
- The 1.5× test: anything that drags at 1.5× will drag at 1×.
- Ideal vlog duration 10–20 min.

**Text on screen / lower thirds:** ≤3 lines at once, high-contrast sans, white with shadow, ~5% safe margin. Location cards: name + country (+ optional price/temp), 2.0–2.5 s, entering on a cut not a fade.

**Transitions to avoid:** star wipes, page curls, glitch packs, cross-dissolve between every clip. Use hard cuts, J/L cuts, whip-pans matched to real motion, match cuts. Max one or two signature transitions per video.

**CTA:** mid-video CTAs at 20–50% of runtime outperform end CTAs (40–60% of viewers gone by the end screen). Structure: soft early prompt (~10 s, one sentence) + one integrated mid CTA (~30%) + strong end CTA. End screens: final 5–20 s, one "next video" + one subscribe element.

**Top channels:** Kara and Nate (high cadence, "real side of travel"), Lost LeBlanc (clean, warm grade, adventure arc), Eva zu Beck (deliberately slow, insight as the hook, proof pacing isn't the only model), Drew Binsky (8–12 min single-question structure).

## 3. Thumbnails and titles (2025–2026)

**Thumbnails:** one dominant subject, 2–3 colours, 3–5 words (many still say ≤3). Faces with clear emotion reportedly +20–30% CTR. Design for the 120 px mobile preview. 2026 trend: simplicity, mobile-first, authenticity. Archetypes: minimalist hero-object, authentic reaction face, before/after, bold colour-block, question. 1280×720, <2 MB, keep faces/text out of bottom-right (timestamp).

**Titles:** 55–70 chars (100 max, ~60 visible on mobile); primary keyword in first 40 chars. Formulas: specific number, curiosity gap, transformation promise, warning, year+action, versus, achievement story. Curiosity gap must be genuinely resolved.

**CTR benchmarks:** average 4–6%; >8% top decile; 9%+ exceptional. Search traffic highest (~12.5%).

**YouTube Test & Compare:** covers titles and thumbnails (Dec 2025); up to 3 variants; runs up to 2 weeks; needs ~1,000–5,000 impressions per variant; **winner chosen by watch time per impression, not CTR**.

**Polish nuance:** Polish words ~15–20% longer than English → front-load, abbreviate. Diacritics (ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż) must render in the thumbnail font — test first. Polish travel-vlog convention: place name + concrete number or shock framing.

## 4. Visual and colour

**Why bright and saturated:** phones, outdoors, partial brightness; flat grades lose separation at 120 px; saturated contrasty images survive VP9/AV1 re-encode better.

**Tasteful YouTube grade:** lift shadows slightly (~2% floor), gentle midtone S-curve, +10–20% saturation, warm highlights/mids toward orange, cool shadows toward teal at ~1/3 trailer strength, protect skin tones, keep grade consistent across uploads.

**Phone HDR footage (critical):** iPhone shoots HLG / Dolby Vision 8.4 by default; naive SDR transcode is grey and washed out. Tone-map:

```
zscale=tin=arib-std-b67:min=bt2020nc:pin=bt2020:rin=tv:t=linear:npl=100,
format=gbrpf32le,
zscale=p=bt709,
tonemap=tonemap=hable:desat=0,
zscale=t=bt709:m=bt709:r=tv,
format=yuv420p
```
Detect via `ffprobe` `color_transfer` = `arib-std-b67` (HLG) or `smpte2084` (PQ). `bt709` → leave alone. `-strict unofficial` helps on some Dolby Vision files.

**Stabilization:** two-pass vidstab: `vidstabdetect=shakiness=8:accuracy=15` then `vidstabtransform=smoothing=30:optzoom=1` (3–8% crop). Stabilize only shots that need it.

**Sharpening:** after scaling/stab, luma only, `unsharp=5:5:0.6:5:5:0.0`; skip for iPhone (already sharpened).

**Vertical in 16:9:** (1) crop-and-pan when source ≥2160 high; (2) blurred pillarbox:
```
split[o][c];[c]scale=ih*16/9:-1,crop=h=iw*9/16,gblur=sigma=20,eq=brightness=-0.08[bg];[bg][o]overlay=(main_w-overlay_w)/2:(main_h-overlay_h)/2
```
(3) side-by-side only with two worthwhile vertical shots.

**Upscaling:** `scale=1920:-2:flags=lanczos` for modest upscales; don't upscale 1080p→4K just for the codec tier.

## 5. Audio

**Loudness:** YouTube normalizes to **−14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP**. Louder gets attenuated; quieter is NOT boosted. Normalize before upload with two-pass loudnorm:
```
# pass 1
-af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.0:LRA=11:print_format=json -f null -
# pass 2
-af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.0:LRA=11:measured_I=..:measured_TP=..:measured_LRA=..:measured_thresh=..:offset=..:linear=true
```

**Dialogue vs music:** music short-term ≈ −28 to −32 LUFS under speech, ≈ −18 to −22 LUFS in the clear (narration at −14 integrated). Duck 8–12 dB.

**Sidechain ducking:**
```
[music][voice_key]sidechaincompress=threshold=0.015:ratio=15:attack=30:release=800:knee=6:makeup=1[ducked];
[ducked][voice]amix=inputs=2:duration=longest:normalize=0
```
Attack 20–30 ms, release 300–800 ms.

**Voice cleanup chain:** `highpass=f=80` → `afftdn=nf=-25` → `deesser` → `acompressor=threshold=-18dB:ratio=3:attack=5:release=120` → `loudnorm`. `arnndn` (RNNoise) better on chaotic backgrounds when the model matches.

**Room tone:** sample the quietest 1 s of each clip, loop it under jump cuts instead of digital silence.

**Music rights:** ~2.2B Content ID claims in 2024, >99% automated, incidental music in public spaces included. Claim → usually "Monetize" policy (video stays, revenue to rightsholder). Strike (3 in 90 days) terminates. Shorts >1 min with an active claim get blocked. Mitigation: duck/replace incidental music aggressively; run YouTube Studio's pre-publish copyright check.

**AI music:** ElevenLabs Music v2 trained on licensed data (Merlin, Kobalt), cleared for commercial use on paid plans (except film/TV/games); **free tier not licensed for monetized use**. Keep licence records.

## 6. Captions / subtitles / on-screen text

- Up to 80% more likely to finish with subtitles; ~69% watch muted in public.
- Shorts with burned-in captions: +15–25% retention. Long-form effect smaller.
- **Recommendation:** upload Polish SRT (toggleable, SEO, enables auto-translate = biggest reach lever) + selective burned-in text for: hook line, location cards, numbers/prices, foreign-language exchanges, bad-audio ranges. Not always-on burned subtitles on the main video.
- Style: bold sans (Inter Bold, Montserrat ExtraBold), white fill, 2–3 px black outline + soft shadow, 3–5 words per chunk, size ≈ 4.5–5.5% of frame height (50–60 px at 1080p), lower third but above the bottom 12%.

## 7. YouTube upload specs (2026)

Official: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171
- MP4, moov atom first (faststart), no edit lists.
- H.264 High Profile, progressive, 2 consecutive B-frames, closed GOP = half the frame rate, CABAC, VBR, 4:2:0.
- AAC-LC 48 kHz 384 kbps stereo.
- BT.709 for SDR. Same frame rate as recorded.
- Bitrate floors: 1080p30 8 Mbps, 1080p60 12; 1440p30 16, 1440p60 24; 2160p30 35–45, 2160p60 53–68. Upload 20–50% above. 2-pass VBR.
- 1440p+ uploads get VP9/AV1 with bigger budgets → sharper even on 1080p displays. Real gain comes from real resolution; if the phone shoots 4K, master at 4K or ≥1440p. Don't upscale purely to game it.
- VFR → CFR on ingest (`-vsync cfr -r 30` or `fps=30`), iPhone often VFR.
- Chapters: first at `0:00`, ≥3 chapters, each ≥10 s, ascending, `m:ss Title` per line in the description. 3–5 for <10 min, 5–10 for 10–30 min. Keyword-rich labels.
- Description: primary keyword + hook in first 200 chars, chapters below, links last. Tags low weight.
- Publishing: Tue–Thu 12:00–15:00 local, secondary 18:00–21:00; publish 2–3 h before the channel's own peak.

## 8. AI-assisted editing (2025–2026)

- Tools: Opus Clip, Descript (edit via transcript), Submagic (captions, silence/filler removal, B-roll, auto-zoom), Captions/Mirage, DaVinci Resolve 20, Gling.ai (closest analogue: cuts silences/mistakes automatically).
- Silence/filler removal cuts 20–40% on talking-head; for travel be conservative: remove silences >0.6 s in piece-to-camera, 250 ms padding, never cut ambient-only B-roll sequences.
- Jump cuts: hide behind B-roll or a 1–2% punch-in.
- **AI-generated B-roll: credibility risk for a travel channel.** Audience: tools OK, laziness not; retention can fall at that moment. YouTube demonetized mass-synthetic channels in Jan 2026. Recommendation: use AI footage only for clearly stylized inserts (maps, illustrations), never as fake travel footage.
- **Disclosure:** YouTube requires disclosing realistic altered/synthetic content (face replacement, synthetic voice narration, altered real events/places). Exempt: colour, filters, stabilization, captions, AI-assisted scripts. Source: https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content/
- **AI voice pickups:** cloning your own voice for occasional pickup lines is mainstream and doesn't trigger the label; if AI voice carries >~20% of narration, disclose. EU AI Act and several US state laws require consent/disclosure.

## Rules to encode in software

**Ingest & normalization**
1. Detect HDR (`color_transfer ∈ {arib-std-b67, smpte2084}`) → apply the zscale/tonemap=hable chain above.
2. VFR → CFR on ingest at the mode of the source fps; log clips whose frame interval varies >5%.
3. Proxy pass: `scale=1280:-2`, crf 26 veryfast for analysis; conform to master at export.

**Structure & pacing**
4. Timeline template markers at 0:07 (promise), 0:30 (pattern interrupt), 3:00 (re-engagement 1), 6:00 (re-engagement 2), `duration−20s` (end screen). Warn if a marker has no beat.
5. Max shot length 4 s before 6:00, 7 s after; rolling avg per 30 s window, warn if >5 s before 6:00.
6. Dead-time detector: no cut/push-in/text/scale change for >12 s → flag.
7. Intro guard: first spoken word must start ≤2.0 s; no logo animation in first 7 s.
8. Ending guard: last 3 s contain no wrap-up phrase (regex on Polish transcript: "dzięki za oglądanie", "do zobaczenia", "na tym kończymy") before the payoff.
9. B-roll ratio: A-roll 30–45% of runtime; any A-roll run >10 s without cutaway → flag.
10. Silence removal: `silencedetect=n=-32dB:d=0.6`, 250 ms padding, fill with room tone, only within A-roll ranges.

**Colour**
11. Base grade after tone-mapping: `curves=all='0/0.02 0.25/0.22 0.5/0.5 0.75/0.79 1/0.99', eq=contrast=1.06:saturation=1.14:gamma=1.02, colorbalance=rs=-0.02:bs=0.04:rh=0.03:bh=-0.03`. Cap saturation 1.20.
12. Skin protection: if face-region hue drifts >8° from 30–40°, halve warmth and re-render.
13. Selective stabilization via vidstabdetect magnitude threshold.
14. Sharpen last, luma only, mild; skip iPhone sources.
15. Vertical handler: crop-and-pan when height ≥2160, else blurred pillarbox with −0.08 brightness.

**Audio**
16. Master `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.0:LRA=11` two-pass.
17. Voice chain: highpass 80 → afftdn → deesser → acompressor.
18. Ducking: sidechaincompress keyed on narration; verify music 14–18 LU under narration.
19. Content ID pre-flight: flag segments with music energy and no licence; operator must duck/replace/accept.
20. Export AAC-LC 384 kbps 48 kHz.

**Text & captions**
21. Safe area: 5% L/R/top, 12% bottom. Font size 0.05 × frame height, white, 3 px outline, shadow. Hard-fail if font lacks Polish glyphs.
22. Polish SRT for upload: 3–5 words per cue, 1.0–4.0 s each. Burn in only hook, location cards, numbers, bad-audio ranges.

**Export & publish**
23. Master: `libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -g fps/2 -bf 2 -coder 1 -b:v tier -pass 2 -movflags +faststart -color_primaries bt709 -color_trc bt709 -colorspace bt709`. Tiers: 1080p30 10 Mbps, 1080p60 15, 1440p30 20, 2160p30 45. Strip edit lists.
24. Deliver at native resolution when ≥1440p; don't upscale purely to reach it.
25. Chapter validator (0:00, ≥3, ≥10 s, ascending, keyword labels).
26. Description builder (keyword+hook in first 200 chars, chapters, links).
27. Title validator: 55–70 chars, keyword in first 40, ≥1 of {number, curiosity gap, superlative, transformation}, no keyword repetition; warn >70 given Polish length.
28. Thumbnail validator: 1280×720, <2 MB, ≤5 words, ≤3 dominant colours, one subject; render a 120 px preview for sign-off.
29. Generate 3 titles + 3 thumbnails per video for Test & Compare; remind: judged on watch time per impression; 2 weeks / ≥1,000 impressions per variant.
30. AI disclosure gate: flag only if photorealistic synthetic footage or synthetic voice over threshold is present.

## Agent editing checklist

**Before the edit**
- Read the retention curves of the last 3 uploads; note the three largest drops.
- Confirm title and thumbnail concept first; every edit decision descends from them.
- Identify the thumbnail moment and the 3:00 / 6:00 beats in the footage log. If missing, say so.

**First 60 seconds**
- Cold open within 2 s; no logo, no greeting, no "so today we're…".
- Thumbnail promise visually confirmed before 0:25.
- Premise in ≤5 s of narration; stakes stated.
- Best-lit footage of the whole video in this window.
- Pattern interrupt at 0:25–0:35.

**Body**
- Arrival/logistics compressed. Re-engagement at ~3:00 and ~6:00.
- No shot >4 s before 6:00 / >7 s after. No 12 s without visual change.
- Every A-roll run <10 s or broken by cutaway. Watched at 1.5×.
- Hard cuts by default; one signature transition max.
- Location card for every new place, 2–2.5 s, on a cut.

**Ending**
- Payoff, then hard cut. No wrap-up monologue before the payoff.
- Final 20 s clean for end screen. Subscribe CTA at ~30% integrated, not stacked at the end.

**Technical QC**
- HDR tone-mapped; consistent colour; skin natural; saturation ≤ +20%.
- −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP; music 14–18 LU under narration; no pumping.
- No unlicensed music including incidental background music.
- Polish diacritics render everywhere. Captions inside safe area.
- MP4, H.264 High, faststart, no edit lists, AAC-LC 384k/48kHz, native resolution.

**Publish**
- Title 55–70 chars, keyword first 40, genuine curiosity gap.
- Thumbnail legible at 120 px; ≤5 words; ≤3 colours; one subject; real emotion.
- 3+3 variants in Test & Compare for 2 weeks.
- Chapters valid; description keyword+hook first 200 chars.
- Polish SRT uploaded (auto-translate).
- AI disclosure only if warranted.
- Schedule Tue–Thu 12:00–15:00 or 2–3 h before channel peak. Run copyright check first.
